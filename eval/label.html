What counts

  • person: any real person other than you, or any part of one (head, body, legs, an arm), even if small, far away, partly hidden, blurry or behind glass.
  • Don't count your own hands, arms, legs or feet.
  • unsure: too tiny or blurry to tell, or people only in posters, photos, screens, statues or reflections. Unsure time isn't scored.
  • Start at the first frame a person shows and end at the last one. Use , . to fix the edges, or the now buttons in the table.
